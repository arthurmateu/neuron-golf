## Can you explain what a black hole is?

```text
A **black hole** is a region of the universe where the gravity is so strong that nothing, including light and other forms of radiation, can escape from it.

Here is a breakdown of how they form, what they look like, and why they are unique:

### 1. The Core Mechanism: The Event Horizon
The defining feature of a black hole is its **event horizon**. This is the boundary (or "point of no return") around the black hole.
*   If you cross this boundary, the escape velocity (the speed needed to break free from gravity) exceeds the speed of light.
*   Since nothing in the universe can travel faster than light, once you cross this point, you cannot get back.

### 2. How They Form
Most black holes are formed from the **collapse of massive stars**.
*   When a star with a mass greater than about 10 times the mass of the Sun runs out of its nuclear fuel
```
